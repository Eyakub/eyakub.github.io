Workout, round two

Two directions on the same app. Plates is bold and colour-coded: every workout has its bumper-plate colour, and your week fills a barbell. Blueprint is a technique sheet: line drawings with joint angles and paths.

All animations
  • The week has no fixed days: 4 sessions, whenever you can. The rotation carries across weeks, so a missed day never skips a workout.
  • Every figure is the same rig, drawn in code. Working muscles light up, and the caption follows the rep.
  • History, body numbers and charts are all sample data.
